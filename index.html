01Story

A systems mindset for better releases.

Five years in test automation and QA, across insurance and healthcare.

I work where test strategy, automation and delivery meet. From reusable modules and data-driven design to parallel execution and defect triage, I build quality into the way teams ship.

I spent four years at Expleo Group building and maintaining Tosca suites for Allianz insurance applications. Today I'm at Optum Global Solutions, UnitedHealth Group, automating end-to-end healthcare scenarios and running 200+ regression test cases every day.
